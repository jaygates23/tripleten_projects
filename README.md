## 🚀 Current Projects

### 🎬 Sprint 1: Movie & TV Show Data Analysis  
**Technologies:** Python, Pandas  
Explored a dataset of movies and TV shows to uncover trends in content type, genre, and release year. Gained hands-on experience with data cleaning, indexing, filtering, and summarization using pandas.

---

### 🛒 Sprint 2: Grocery Shopping Behavior – Instacart  
**Technologies:** Python, Pandas, Data Visualization  
Analyzed customer behavior from the Instacart dataset. Identified peak shopping times, reorder patterns, and product trends to support marketing and inventory decisions.

---

### 📈 Sprint 3: Revenue Attribution – Megaline Mobile Plans  
**Technologies:** Python, Pandas, Statistical Testing  
Performed hypothesis testing and revenue analysis to compare two mobile plans. Recommended business strategies based on customer behavior and revenue insights.

---

### 🎮 Video Game Sales Forecasting – ICE  
**Technologies:** Python, Pandas, Hypothesis Testing, Data Visualization  
Conducted market research on global video game sales across platforms and regions. Identified high-performing genres and regional sales drivers to inform strategic publishing decisions.

---

### 📱 Sprint 7: Mobile Plan Recommendation – Megaline  
**Technologies:** Scikit-learn, Gradient Boosting, Random Forest  
Developed a classification model to recommend optimal mobile plans. Gradient Boosting achieved the highest accuracy, with internet usage as the top predictor of plan suitability.

---

### 🔁 Sprint 8: Customer Churn Prediction – Beta Bank  
**Technologies:** LightGBM, XGBoost, Class Imbalance Handling  
Built a churn prediction model with strong recall performance. Identified key churn indicators and supported customer retention efforts with actionable insights.

---

### 🛢️ Sprint 9: Oil Well Profit Optimization – OilyGiant  
**Technologies:** Linear Regression, Bootstrapping, Profit Simulation  
Optimized region selection for oil drilling investments. Used predictive modeling and financial simulation to recommend the most profitable and low-risk regions.

---

### 🔧 Sprint 10: Gold Recovery Predictions – OilyGiant  
**Technologies:** Linear Regression, Bootstrapping, Profit Simulation  
This project focused on developing machine learning models to predict gold recovery at two key stages of the mineral processing pipeline: the rougher stage and the final purification stage.
